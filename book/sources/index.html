How to read this room

Evidence has its own ritual.

A citation is a small door left open in the wall of the sentence.

Each chapter of the finished volume will carry short notes at the point of use, then return the reader here for the full bibliographic trail. Historical claims, analytical claims, pharmacological claims, surviving objects, and house interpretations are kept visibly distinct.

Editorial method

Four kinds of authority

  • Primary record: archival object, original paper, legal text, historical testimony, or museum record.
  • Scholarly synthesis: a monograph, review, institutional history, or peer-reviewed interpretation.
  • House observation: batch actual, sensory sheet, louche photograph, garden log, or laboratory measurement.
  • Interpretation: a symbolic, literary, musical, or visual correspondence made by Nowhere's End.

Citation promise

What the book will not blur

  • Historical use is not proof of modern efficacy.
  • A molecule’s presence is not proof of a physiological effect at beverage dose.
  • A cultural story is not a clinical finding.
  • A commissioned plate is not an archival photograph.
  • A house formula remains provisional until its actuals are measured.
