The Print Manifest

Sequence · plate · caption · proof

The book is not finished when the words are written. It is finished when folio, image, caption, source, alt text, and production note agree.

Production brief200 folios

A large-format book with a visible chain of custody

Every beautiful page must be able to answer: what is this, where did it come from, and how should it be printed?

Format

9 × 12 in trim

Full-bleed plates where possible; generous caption margins; sewn binding; warm uncoated stock for text and botanical work.

Image

Plate-led rhythm

Sixteen anchor plates divide the six books. Each image is marked as house interpretation, sourced history, reconstruction, or analytical diagram.

Proof

Web proof → print proof

Current raster assets are approved for composition and captioning. Final prepress export must confirm resolution, bleed, color profile, and paper simulation.

Caption preflightRequired

Before export

  • Caption identifies subject and image status.
  • Alt text describes what is visibly present.
  • Historical claims link to a source.
  • House interpretation is labeled as interpretation.
  • Scientific diagrams state what they do not prove.
  • Crop preserves plant, glass, apparatus, or object identity.
Large-format preflightRequired

Before print proof

  • Confirm 9 × 12 in trim, bleed, and safe caption margins.
  • Convert final images to the printer’s color profile.
  • Confirm effective resolution at placed size.
  • Check folio numbers and plate references against this manifest.
  • Inspect dark green shadow detail and louche yellow separation.
  • Proof uncoated stock before approving full run.

Status

v0.9 is a production manuscript

The text, plates, captions, and source boundaries are now organized as a print object. The final gate is physical prepress: resolution, color, bleed, paper, and page-turn proof—not a new conceptual outline.
