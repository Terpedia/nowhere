# Absinthe COA reconciliation

The local COA artifact lists 27 volatile rows and reports a summary of 91.2 area% identified material plus 8.8% unidentified peaks. The curated study inventory currently contains 26 volatile rows and three nonvolatile profile entries, for 29 analysis compounds.

The omitted COA row is p-cymene (CAS 99-87-6). It is excluded from the current receptor and structure analysis until its identity, source attribution, and relationship to the existing terpene records are reconciled. This omission is intentional and is disclosed in the manuscript; the 29-compound map must not be described as an exhaustive transcription of every COA row.

The displayed numeric area values in the source table do not reproduce its 91.2% summary exactly because of rounding or an upstream source-table inconsistency. The manuscript therefore reports the source summary and does not substitute a recomputed total for it.
