Is absinthe a different kind of drunk—and is it psychedelic?

A critical evidence map of 5-HT2A pharmacology, absinthe chemistry, and competing neural mechanisms.

Current conclusionThe available evidence does not establish a reproducibly different intoxication profile from an ethanol-matched spirit or a classic serotonergic psychedelic mechanism. The evidence supports candidate non-5-HT2A modulation—especially GABA-A inhibitory tone and TRPA1 sensory signaling—without a supported direct 5-HT2A edge.

Evidence boundary

Terpedia supplies the inventory, chemical records, target panel, structure joins, and provenance snapshots. External primary literature supplies the reported receptor and human-effect evidence. Missing structure or receptor joins are reported as annotation gaps, not biological inactivity. This site is a read-only presentation layer; the repository and tagged releases are the canonical source.
